# Steamon Trainers

The trainers of the Steamon SMP, for [Radical Cobblemon Trainers](https://modrinth.com/mod/rctmod).

You step out of a rift onto Steamos and join one of three teams: **Spark**, the inventors, **Relic**, the treasure hunters, or **Might**, the warriors. The same story plays out in each version, from your first duel to the fight against Team Flare and the three leaders, with rivals, the Lanugo and a few surprises on the way. The original **Steamon League**, with its 8 Gym Leaders, Elite Four and Champion, is still part of the pack.

Early access: the new story trainers have not been played in game yet, feedback is welcome.

## Good to know

- Needs Cobblemon 1.8, RCTMod 0.19.2-beta and RCTAPI. Mega Showdown is recommended.
- The late fights go above level 100: raise `maxPokemonLevel` in the Cobblemon config.
- Start a story with the Trainer Association or `/rctmod player set series steamos_spark` (or `steamos_relic`, `steamos_might`).
- Built for the [Steamon](https://modrinth.com/modpack/steamon) modpack.
